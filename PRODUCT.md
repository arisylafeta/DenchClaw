# DenchClaw product

DenchClaw is ReBattery's internal workspace for Ari and Alex: a CRM, an inbox, and an agent chat where Hermes profiles (Orchas, Devon, Vangrid and others) do the work. It runs at crm.rebattery.io.

- **Users:** two founders working all day inside it, on desktop and phone. They know the product intimately and notice small regressions fast.
- **Mode:** Operate. People complete tasks. Scanability, consistency and speed beat expression. Brand lives in precise details, not decoration.
- **Job of the chat:** let a founder direct agents and review their work with as little chrome as possible. The assistant's words are the content; everything else stays quiet.
- **Voice:** direct, warm, brief. Lead with the result.
- **Constraints:** Next.js app in `apps/web`, Tailwind with the CSS tokens in `apps/web/app/globals.css`, lucide icons. Synthetic data only when testing. No production data in browser QA.
- **Taste contract:** `DESIGN.md`. The current chat is approved and is the reference for all new work.
